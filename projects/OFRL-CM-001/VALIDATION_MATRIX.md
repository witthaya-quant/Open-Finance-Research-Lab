## 1. Validation rules

Use only these states:
- **PASS:** acceptance criteria were executed and met; evidence is linked.
- **FAIL:** a check was executed and did not meet its criteria.
- **NOT RUN:** the check has not been executed.
- **BLOCKED:** a prerequisite or decision is missing.
- **N/A:** the check does not apply to the stated scope; explain why.

A green metadata workflow does not prove mathematical correctness. Evidence maturity, implementation status, and validation status are tracked separately.

## 3. Initial-pilot structural checks

The official Pressure Points page was accessed on 2026-11-05. The visible source contains:
- One leading marker.
- 75 distinct uppercase-letter sequences.
- 19 emoji clues.
- A terminal notation shown as “(11 12)”.

A structural check of the visible sequence block found that every sequence is internally alphabetically ordered, the list is lexicographically ordered, and the 75 sequences contain 504 letters in total. The CM-T0007 audit separately checked 48 distinct candidate assignments, found 25 exact and 23 residual-bearing comparisons, and left 27 sequences plus Ø unassigned; 28 = 7×4 was arithmetic alignment only. H-016 historically left 23 sequences plus Ø and six emojis at the pre-H-017 checkpoint. H-017 later supplies a conditional third-party 19-roster map across all 76 visible entries; this is not a source-accepted mapping and does not close VAL-014 or VAL-015. The length distribution and detailed residual register are recorded in PROBLEM_FORMALIZATION.md.

These checks establish representation-level properties only; they do not resolve puzzle semantics.
