**Benchmark ID:** OFRL-IB-001-BM-002  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Model:** V1 synthetic Case A, schema 0.1.0  
**Period coverage:** FY2026–FY2030  
**Expected-value coverage:** 32 metrics × 5 years = 160 exact rational outputs  
**Status:** Arithmetic benchmark implemented; independent finance/accounting sign-off remains open

## 1. Purpose and independence

BM-002 extends the independently derived FY2026 benchmark (BM-001) to the complete five-year Case A forecast horizon. The immutable expected values are stored in [the golden dataset](golden/case-a-five-year.v0.1.0.json) as reduced numerator/denominator strings.

Expected values were derived from the published V1 equations and canonical input fixture through a separate exact-rational calculation path. They were not generated by invoking the Rust `forecast<N>()` routine or by reading outputs from any candidate backend. The test invokes the Rust exact-rational implementation only for the *actual* values and compares those values with the independently stored constants.

The three numeric candidates in the comparative POC still share one Rust formula implementation. BM-002 adds an independent expected-output dataset so a defect in that shared implementation can be detected across every Case A forecast year and metric.

This benchmark is not an accounting opinion. It checks the implementation against the stated V1 conventions; it does not certify that those conventions are appropriate for a particular entity, jurisdiction, accounting framework or investment decision.

## 3. Independently applied formula sequence

For each year (t), starting with FY2025 closing balances:

**Operating statement**
- Revenue = Prior revenue × (1 + revenue growth)
- COGS = Revenue × COGS percentage
- Operating expenses = Revenue × operating-expense percentage
- EBITDA = Revenue − COGS − operating expenses
- EBIT = EBITDA − depreciation
- EBT = EBIT − interest expense
- Tax = max(0, EBT × tax rate)
- Net income = EBT − tax

**Working capital**
- AR = Revenue × DSO / 365
- Inventory = COGS × DIO / 365
- AP = COGS × DPO / 365
- NWC = AR + inventory − AP
- Change in NWC = Closing NWC − prior closing NWC

**PP&E**
- CapEx = Revenue × CapEx percentage
- Closing gross PP&E = Opening gross PP&E + CapEx
- Average gross PP&E = (Opening gross PP&E + Closing gross PP&E) / 2
- Depreciation = min(Average gross PP&E × depreciation rate, Opening net PP&E + CapEx)
- Closing accumulated depreciation = Opening accumulated depreciation + depreciation
- Closing net PP&E = Closing gross PP&E − closing accumulated depreciation

**Debt, dividends and cash flow**
- Closing debt = Opening debt + drawdowns − repayments
- Interest = ((Opening debt + Closing debt) / 2) × interest rate
- Dividends = max(0, net income) × payout ratio
- CFO = Net income + depreciation − change in NWC
- CFI = −CapEx
- CFF = Drawdowns − repayments − dividends
- Closing cash = Opening cash + CFO + CFI + CFF

**Equity and Balance Sheet**
- Retained earnings = Opening retained earnings + net income − dividends
- Total assets = Closing cash + AR + inventory + net PP&E
- Total liabilities and equity = AP + closing debt + share capital + retained earnings
- Balance Sheet difference = Total assets − total liabilities and equity

Each year rolls forward the prior year's independently calculated revenue, cash, NWC, debt, gross PP&E, accumulated depreciation and retained earnings. Share capital remains constant. No automatic financing or balancing plug is inserted.

## 5. Automated verification contract

The test `case_a_five_year_forecast_matches_independent_exact_golden` enforces:

1. Exact fixture schema/model identity.
2. Exactly five ordered forecast periods, FY2026–FY2030.
3. Exactly 32 metrics per period, with no missing or unexpected output.
4. Exact numerator/denominator equality for all 160 outputs.
5. Exact zero Balance Sheet difference in every period.
6. A fixed coverage count of 160 so accidental test-scope reduction fails CI.

No tolerance or two-decimal quantization is used by BM-002. Finite-precision candidates are still assessed separately against the exact-rational reference; they are not expected to reproduce repeating fractions identically.

## 6. Limitations and remaining review

BM-002 covers one synthetic Case A. It does not yet cover all edge scenarios: loss-making periods, zero activity/denominators, zero debt, full debt repayment, zero CapEx, negative cash, day/rate boundaries and invalid repayment paths. These require additional versioned benchmarks.

This benchmark also does not set the business-appropriate maximum company scale, minimum material amount, input precision, maximum intermediate range, accumulated error budget, reporting rounding or reconciliation tolerance. Those remain Numeric Profile decisions.
