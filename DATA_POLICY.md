# OFRL Data Policy

## Purpose

OFRL publishes research that can be inspected and reproduced without disregarding the rights of data providers, issuers, contributors, or individuals. Data provenance and permitted use are part of the research method—not an administrative step after analysis.

## 1. Data classes

### A. OFRL-created synthetic data

Synthetic datasets independently generated by OFRL for examples, demonstrations, and research fixtures may be released under CC BY 4.0 by default, with:

- A clear synthetic-data label.
- A description of the generation method and assumptions.
- A schema, units, time conventions, and field definitions.
- A deterministic seed or generation configuration where relevant.
- A statement that the values do not represent actual company, client, or market observations.

Synthetic data must not be generated by simply perturbing restricted or confidential source data where that process could disclose or reconstruct the source.

### B. Third-party data

Third-party data remains under its original licence, contract, terms of service, and applicable legal restrictions. OFRL will not substitute Apache-2.0 or CC BY 4.0 for a provider's terms.

Before redistribution, confirm that the rights explicitly cover the intended publication and reuse. A free download, public website, exchange display, public filing, or API response does not by itself establish redistribution rights.

### C. Public filings and issuer financial statements

Public availability is not a blanket redistribution permission. Prefer citations, source links, filing identifiers, and reproducible retrieval instructions. Store copies only where source terms and applicable law permit.

When figures are manually transcribed or normalized, document the original filing, reporting period, units, accounting basis, restatements, mapping choices, and adjustments.

### D. Curated and derived datasets

Cleaning, transforming, combining, or calculating metrics from third-party data does not automatically remove upstream restrictions. Assess copyright, database rights, contract terms, confidentiality, and any restrictions attached to the source.

Where redistribution rights are unclear, publish methodology and a data manifest rather than the underlying records.

## 2. Required data manifest

Every research project using non-trivial external or synthetic data should provide a manifest containing:

| Field | Required description |
|---|---|
| Dataset ID and version | Stable project identifier and version |
| Data classification | Synthetic, public-source, licensed, restricted, or derived |
| Provider / origin | Named source or generation method |
| Source reference | URL, filing identifier, instrument identifier, or internal synthetic generator |
| Access date | Date the source was accessed or data generated |
| Coverage | Entities, instruments, geography, and date range |
| Frequency and timezone | Observation frequency and time convention |
| Schema and units | Field names, definitions, currency, scale, and units |
| Transformations | Adjustments, filters, mappings, and exclusions |
| Licence / terms | Exact licence or source terms and version where available |
| Redistribution | Permitted, prohibited, or not established |
| Attribution | Required source citation and attribution text |
| Integrity | Optional checksum or content hash for reproducibility |

## 3. Data-release gate

A dataset may be committed to a public repository only when all of the following pass:

1. Source and provenance are identified.
2. The applicable rights and terms have been reviewed.
3. The intended redistribution and commercial-use conditions are understood.
4. Required attribution and notices are included.
5. Personal, confidential, client, employer, and restricted information is excluded.
6. The data manifest is complete.
7. The dataset's licence is stated without overriding upstream rights.

If any gate fails or remains unknown, do not publish the data file. Use a manifest, source link, schema, and lawful retrieval procedure where possible.

## 4. Default licensing

- Original OFRL synthetic research datasets: CC BY 4.0, unless designated as integral software test fixtures.
- Synthetic fixtures integral to software tests: Apache-2.0 where clearly identified.
- Third-party datasets: source licence and terms only.
- Data manifests and explanatory documentation: CC BY 4.0.
- Data-processing code, parsers, and validation utilities: Apache-2.0.

The repository's LICENSE, LICENSE-docs, and LICENSE_POLICY.md describe the broader content-type licensing model.

## 5. No implied data rights

OFRL does not warrant that data from an external source is accurate, complete, current, or suitable for any use. A research result must distinguish source facts, normalized values, assumptions, and derived outputs.

This policy is an operational research standard, not legal advice.
